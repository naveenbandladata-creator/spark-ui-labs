# Ep 6 - Spot the Problem #1: answers

**Q1.** Around 40 jobs (each `count()` and each `collect()` makes 2 jobs with AQE on). One report should need 1-2.

**Q2.** The `for region in range(10)` loop. Every pass runs two actions, `count()` and `collect()`,
and each action re-reads and re-filters all 2 million rows from scratch.
In the Jobs tab, 20 jobs are named `collect at ...ep06_spot_the_problem_1.py:<line>`, which points straight at the code.
The other 20 have a cryptic name (`$anonfun$withThreadLocalCaptured...`): those are the `count()` calls.
Cryptic names like that are why Ep 2 teaches `setJobDescription`.

**Q3.** Both, but the Event Timeline shows the pattern: short bursts of executor work separated by gaps.
The gaps are the driver (printing, the `sleep`, sending the next job). Executors are idle during them.

**Q4.** Let Spark do all regions in one pass:

```python
report = orders.groupBy("region").agg(F.count("*").alias("rows"), F.sum("amount").alias("total"))
report.show()
```

One action, one pass over the data, all ten regions at once.
